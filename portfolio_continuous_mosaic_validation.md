## Shared-field correction

The final desktop mosaic is a single 12-column by 9-row field. It is intentionally filled by three 2×2 feature tiles, thirty standard 1×2 portrait tiles, six 1×1 portrait detail tiles, and fifteen 2×1 landscape tiles. This equals all 108 cells of the field, leaving no blank cells at the bottom or within the Portfolio.

The ordering now deliberately lets a small number of later-range images occupy seams beside the preceding range. Each image remains inside its approved range—01–09, 10–15, 16–33, 34–42, or 43–54—but the ranges no longer restart as visibly top-aligned blocks. The 2px grid gap is the only spacing used within the mosaic and between implied group transitions.
