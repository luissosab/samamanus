The replacement site will use original full-resolution OneDrive images as image assets. The current screenshot-derived archive imagery is to be removed from the visitor-facing experience once equivalent high-resolution seasonal imagery has been selected and integrated.

## Inspected original asset

`aw22-alpaca-knit-dress-5-771.jpg` was retrieved from the OneDrive viewer as a 4160 × 6240 pixel original. It is a portrait-oriented, full-length rear view of a model in a pale textured sleeveless knit dress against a pale studio background. The image has meaningful negative space at the top, clear knit texture in the upper garment, and a quiet editorial scale suitable for a large vertical collection plate rather than a thumbnail.
